Part Behaviour
Screen readers Decorative: the host is aria-hidden, holds no text, takes no pointer and is never focusable. The listeners on the parent are passive and stop nothing, so every control over the field behaves as before.
Touch A moving finger pushes nothing, nothing is prevented and touch-action is left alone, so a scroll that starts over the region scrolls. A tap sends a ripple, as a click does.
Reduced motion Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce one still frame of the whole field is drawn, with no push, no ripple and no intro, and nothing moves; a preference that flips mid-flight stops the loop at once. Under forced-colors: active nothing is drawn.
Contrast A line is a hairline laid in the muted ink at well under full coverage, so the text over it reads as it does on the plain surface; the accent shows only where a line is pushed.
No WebGL Without WebGL nothing is drawn; a lost context is let go and rebuilt when the browser restores it.
Server The empty canvas is rendered, absolutely positioned, so the browser's first draw shifts no layout.
