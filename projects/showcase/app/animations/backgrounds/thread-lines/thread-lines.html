Part Behaviour
Screen readers Decorative: the host is aria-hidden, holds no text, takes no pointer and is never focusable. The listeners on the parent are passive and stop nothing, so every control over the ribbon behaves as before.
Touch A finger is ignored: it parts nothing, nothing is prevented and touch-action is left alone, so a scroll that starts over the region scrolls.
Reduced motion Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce one still frame of the closed ribbon is drawn and nothing moves; a preference that flips mid-flight stops the loop at once. Under forced-colors: active nothing is drawn.
Contrast A strand is a hairline laid in the body's ink at well under full coverage, brightest in the middle of the bundle and faded at its edges and ends, so the text over it reads as it does on the plain surface.
No WebGL Without WebGL nothing is drawn; a lost context is let go and rebuilt when the browser restores it.
Server The empty canvas is rendered, absolutely positioned, so the browser's first draw shifts no layout.
