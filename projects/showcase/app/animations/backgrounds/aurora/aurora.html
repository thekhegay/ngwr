Part Behaviour
Screen readers Decorative: the host is aria-hidden, holds no text, takes no pointer and is never focusable. The listeners on the parent are passive and stop nothing, so every control over the curtain behaves as before.
Touch A finger is ignored, nothing is prevented and touch-action is left alone, so a scroll that starts over the region scrolls.
Reduced motion Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce one still frame of the whole curtain is drawn, at rest, with no drift and no intro, and nothing moves; a preference that flips mid-flight stops the loop at once. Under forced-colors: active nothing is drawn.
Contrast The root the curtain hangs from is dim, so text near the anchored edge reads over a faint tint. On a light surface the wash is pale throughout, so text keeps its contrast wherever it sits: measured under the first demo above, over several frames and at the worst pixel, the heading keeps 15:1 and the lead 5.7:1. On a dark surface the brightest part sits just short of the free edge: the heading never falls under 11:1 and the lead keeps 6.5:1 over most of its pixels, but where a bright ray crosses the lead's lowest line the worst pixel is about 2.2:1. Muted text that must sit under the glow in dark wants the other edge, a shorter height or a lower glow.
No WebGL Without WebGL nothing is drawn; a lost context is let go and rebuilt when the browser restores it.
Server The empty canvas is rendered, absolutely positioned, so the browser's first draw shifts no layout.
