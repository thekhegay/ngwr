Part Behaviour
Keyboard An activation from Enter or Space is a click with detail === 0: the burst comes from the host's centre. The listener is passive and prevents nothing.
Screen readers Each burst's layer is aria-hidden, holds no text and takes no pointer.
Reduced motion Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce nothing plays; under forced-colors: active nothing listens, since the stylesheet paints no stroke. A preference that flips mid-burst leaves an invisible layer, which a timer takes out.
Server Nothing runs: the listener is added after the first render, in the browser only.
