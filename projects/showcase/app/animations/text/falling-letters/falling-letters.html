Click this line and watch it fall.

Lands on the floor of the arena, not of its own parent.

Part Behaviour
Screen readers The authored nodes are moved into an invisible wrapper that holds exactly their space, still the real text for a screen reader and the clipboard. The pieces are aria-hidden, out of the selection and under no pointer, so a button a letter falls across stays clickable.
Reduced motion Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce a click does nothing, and a preference that flips mid-fall puts the text back at once. The demos on this page follow the same setting.
Layout The line never moves: the pieces are positioned from the target's own box and move by transform alone, so nothing under them reflows. When the last letter is back, the same node objects go back where they were, so a binding inside the text keeps working.
Server Nothing happens on the server or when the target has no box. The click listener is added in the browser, after the first render.
