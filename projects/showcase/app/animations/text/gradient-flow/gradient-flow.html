Part Behaviour
Screen readers Nothing changes: no split, no copy, no pseudo-element. The gradient is a background clipped to the glyphs, so what is announced, selected and copied is the text as authored.
Reduced motion Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce the gradient stays and nothing moves: it is colour, not motion. The demos on this page follow the same setting.
Forced colours Under forced-colors: active the text is plain, in the system colour, and the directive does not run.
Server The server paints the final colours before any script arrives. The drift starts in the browser, after the first render, and only where IntersectionObserver exists.
Direction The wave travels the reading direction: under dir="rtl" it drifts right to left.
