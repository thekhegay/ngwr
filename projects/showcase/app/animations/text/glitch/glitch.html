Part Behaviour
Screen readers The text is never touched: its node stays where it is, with its bindings. The copies are aria-hidden spans appended for the length of a burst and removed after it, and they sit outside the selection, so a copy of the page gives the text once.
Flashing Two bursts start at least 1.3 s apart, so the text shows at most one opposing pair of luminance changes in any one second, where WCAG 2.3.1 allows three. The copies are faint tints over the glyphs, never a strobe, and the text reads in its own colour at every frame.
Reduced motion Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce nothing plays and the stylesheet paints no copy. The demos on this page follow the same setting.
Forced colours Under forced-colors: active no copy is painted and the directive does not run.
Server The server renders the text as it is. The cycle starts in the browser, after the first render, and only where IntersectionObserver exists.
