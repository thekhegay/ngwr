A wider, slower tear

Part Behaviour
Screen readers The text is never touched: its node stays where it is, with its bindings. The copies are aria-hidden spans appended while the pointer is over the text and removed when it leaves, and they sit outside the selection, so a copy of the page gives the text once.
Flashing The line is cut into twelve thin bands and each copy lights each of its six bands once per cycle, so no point of the text is lit more than once a cycle by either copy. With the cycle at a second or more, any one second shows a point at most two opposing pairs of luminance changes, where WCAG 2.3.1 allows three. The copies are faint tints over thin strips of glyphs, never a strobe, and the text reads in its own colour at every frame.
Reduced motion Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce nothing plays and the stylesheet paints no copy. The demos on this page follow the same setting.
Forced colours Under forced-colors: active no copy is painted and the directive does not run.
Server The server renders the text as it is. The listeners are added in the browser, after the first render. Nothing plays while the document is hidden.
