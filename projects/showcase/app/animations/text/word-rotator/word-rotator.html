Part Behaviour
Screen readers The slot is aria-hidden and a visually hidden phrase beside it names every word once, so a reader hears the sentence with "A, B and C" in it and never a word mid-flight. The phrase is out of the selection, so a copy of the sentence gives the word on show.
Motion that stops The list plays once and stops on the first word, as WCAG 2.2.2 asks of content that moves on its own; loop lifts that. A pointer over the sentence or focus in it pauses the hold, and so does the host leaving the viewport or the document being hidden.
Reduced motion Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce nothing swaps, not even without motion: the first word stands as text. The demos on this page follow the same setting.
Server The server renders the first word as text, and so does a browser without IntersectionObserver. The cycle starts in the browser once most of the host is in view.
Layout At rest the slot holds nothing but the text, so the line reads, breaks, selects and copies as if the word were typed there. While swapping it is clipped to its line, so a letter on its way never crosses a neighbouring line.
