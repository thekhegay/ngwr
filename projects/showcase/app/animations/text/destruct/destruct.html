Part Behaviour
Screen readers Nothing changes: no copy, no canvas, no split. The filter is paint over the host, so selection, copy, find-in-page and the accessible tree see the element as it was.
Reduced motion Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce nothing mounts, and a preference that flips mid-run clears the filter at once. The demos on this page follow the same setting.
Forced colours Under forced-colors: active no filter is mounted and the text stays as it is.
Server The server renders the text as it is. The filter is mounted in the browser, after the first render, and the text renders exactly as without the effect until a pointer or focus arrives.
