A band of light across the glyphs.

 On a fill
Part Behaviour
Screen readers Nothing changes: no split, no copy, no pseudo-element content. The band is paint over the text's own glyphs, so what is announced, selected and copied is the text as authored.
Reduced motion Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce the cycle never starts and the stylesheet paints no band. The demos on this page follow the same setting.
Forced colours Under forced-colors: active the band is not painted and the directive does not run, so the label keeps the system colour.
Server The server renders the label as it is. The cycle starts in the browser, after the first render, and only where IntersectionObserver exists.
Direction The band travels the reading direction: under dir="rtl" it crosses right to left.
