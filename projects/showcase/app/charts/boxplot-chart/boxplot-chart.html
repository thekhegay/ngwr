Mark Means Here
Box The first quartile to the third: the middle half of the values. 16 to 23
Line in the box The median. 19
Whiskers The farthest values within 1.5 × the box's height (the IQR) of the box. 12 and 27
Dots Outliers: values past the whiskers' reach. 48, past 23 + 1.5 × 7
Part Behaviour
Plot One role="img", named by ariaLabel. No tab stop.
Values Only in the tooltip, unless dataTable is on.
Groups Named under their boxes. Colour carries nothing the name does not.
Tooltip Pointer only. Escape closes it.
Forced colours Every mark is drawn as an outline in the system text colour.
