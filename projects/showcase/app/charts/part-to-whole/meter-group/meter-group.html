Part Behaviour
Bar One role="progressbar", named by ariaLabel. It announces the total of the segments shown, written as the legend writes a value.
Segments Announce nothing of their own. The legend names each one with its value.
Legend A real list. A hidden segment's row is struck through, and says so to a screen reader.
Toggle row With toggleableLegend, a <button aria-pressed>. Pressed means shown. Tab moves between rows, Space or Enter toggles.
States The empty and loading messages are a role="status"; a failed load is a role="alert".
Tooltip Pointer only. The legend carries the same values.
Forced colours The segments keep their colours, and the track gets an outline.
