Part Behaviour
Drawing One role="img", named by ariaLabel. The tiles announce nothing.
Numbers In the tooltip, which is pointer only, and in the table dataTable adds.
Groups Told apart by their name and by their tiles sitting inside their own rectangle, not only by colour.
Tile edges A 1px line in the surface colour, which every palette colour holds 3:1 against.
Names Black or white, picked per tile. Left out where a tile would cut one to a few letters. The tooltip and the table carry the whole path.
Values Never cut: a value wider than its tile is left out. The tooltip and the table carry it whole.
Forced colours The fills give way to the system palette; every tile keeps an outline in the text colour.
States The empty and loading messages are a role="status"; a failed load is a role="alert".
Keyboard No tab stop.
