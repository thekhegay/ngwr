Put provideWrI18n, the base catalogs and the loader in the application's root providers. A route's providers do not reach the root service, so there they do nothing and dev mode warns. A lazy area switches the language with inject(WrI18n).use('de'): the switch is app-wide, and the locale must be in the root availableLocales. The area's own keys load as a scope.
