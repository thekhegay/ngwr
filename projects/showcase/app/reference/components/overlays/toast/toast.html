  • The outlet fills its nearest positioned ancestor: give the container position: relative.
  • Each name is a stack of its own: its maxStack, its hold on hover and focus, its “Close all”. The global stack still opens on top.
  • Past maxStack the oldest toast is dropped, where the global stack queues the newest. A stream of thousands a minute stays at maxStack toasts and timers.
  • A toast sent while its outlet is not in the page waits, without counting down, and shows when the outlet appears.
  • The outlet sets no z-index. Its order against its siblings is your own CSS, and clicks pass through it wherever no toast is.

Since v15, closing a toast from the keyboard moves focus to the Close button of the toast that takes its place, else the one before it. Closing the last one returns focus to where it was before it entered the stack.

withToast() in a route's providers is ignored: set it at bootstrap, or pass the value per call.
