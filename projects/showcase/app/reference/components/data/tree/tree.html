Key Does
Alt + Enter Picks the row up
Up, Down Moves it among its siblings
Right, Left Into the row above as its last child, or out to follow its parent (swapped under RTL)
Enter Drops it
Escape Puts it back

A held row stays where it is until you apply the move; the indicator and a live region say where it would land. Dragging is off while a filter is set, and for rows that loadChildren supplied.

Key Does
ArrowDown, ArrowUp Next or previous visible row. On the overlay trigger: opens the panel; once it is open, moves focus to the cursor row.
Tab On the trigger of an open panel, moves into it. In the panel, closes it and moves on.
Escape Closes the overlay panel, focus on the trigger.
ArrowRight Opens a branch, or moves to its first child.
ArrowLeft Closes a branch, or moves to the parent.
Home, End First or last visible row.
Type a letter Moves to the next visible row that starts with what you typed; the same letter again cycles. It opens no branch. Works from the trigger of an open panel too.
Enter, Space Selects the row. With Ctrl or Cmd, adds it to the selection.
Alt+Enter On a draggable tree, picks the row up: the arrows move it, Enter drops it, Escape puts it back.
