Read-only

A readonly calendar keeps its tab stop, its arrows and its header. Only the pick is refused. disabled takes it out of the tab order.

Day view
Key Does
ArrowLeft ArrowRight Previous / next day. Swapped under dir="rtl".
ArrowUp ArrowDown Same weekday, one week back / forward.
Home End First / last day of the week, for the locale.
PageUp PageDown Previous / next month.
Shift + PageUp Shift + PageDown Previous / next year.
Enter Space Picks the day.
Month and year views
Key Does
ArrowLeft ArrowRight Previous / next chip. Swapped under dir="rtl".
ArrowUp ArrowDown One row up / down.
Home End First / last chip on the page.
Enter Space Picks the chip.

ariaLabel names the day grid of a calendar outside a form field. Each built-in name takes a per-instance override: prevMonthLabel, nextMonthLabel, prevYearLabel, nextYearLabel, prevYearsLabel, nextYearsLabel, monthListLabel and yearListLabel.
