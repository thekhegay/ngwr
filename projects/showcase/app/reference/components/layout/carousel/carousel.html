  • With autoplay, turn on pauseButton: WCAG 2.2.2 asks for a way to pause moving content. The button is the carousel's first tab stop, named pauseLabel or playLabel for what a press does next.
  • The pointer over the carousel and focus inside it hold the slides, and leaving resumes them. A press on pause holds them until a press on play.
  • Under prefers-reduced-motion autoplay waits for a press on play.
  • A move by an arrow, a dot, a swipe or next(), prev() and goTo() is announced as "Slide 3 of 5", from the carousel.position catalog key. An autoplay tick is not, and the live region is aria-live="off" while the slides advance on their own.
  • The dots sit 24px apart, centre to centre, and each takes a press up to 8px around it. A smaller --wr-carousel-dots-gap gives that up.
