• Each step is a modal dialog named by its title and the progress line, and described by its text, so a screen reader reads all three when the step opens.
  • Focus opens on the card itself, with no ring, and is trapped in it. Pass { autoFocus: 'first-tabbable' } as the second argument of start() to open on Next, or false to leave focus where it is.
  • Escape ends the tour, and focus returns to where it was.
