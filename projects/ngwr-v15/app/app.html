How strong should a control's border be?

The one open call on this page. Everything else is a report: the ramp, the intents and the neutral hue are settled and shipped.

The two rows outlined in red further down, in Borders, are the choice. The scale's own next step reads 2.56:1 where WCAG 1.4.11 asks 3:1 of anything that identifies a control. Reaching it needs a colour darker than the ramp's rhythm, so every control edge would read visibly heavier than every other line on the page.

Flip the theme with the button above — every number is measured against the live canvas, not transcribed.

Neutral hue — decided: gray

Slate shipped before this and was the bluest of the five — 22 points of blue over red on the hairline, which is what read as light blue on an outline. gray is half that at 10, and the dark ramp was re-tuned to the same hue at each step's own luminance: muted text went from 30 to 20, body text from 13 to 6. Contrast did not move — the five families land within 0.02 of each other at the hairline, and the re-tune preserved every luminance to five decimal places.
