Neutral ramp

Ours is read out of the compiled theme, so this column is whatever the library actually ships, and the counterpart flips with the theme — a dark theme reads the ramp from the other end. The ratio is against the live canvas. A row says so when the two sides match to the byte; the two red rows are steps we do not have, and that is where a 3:1 control border would live.
