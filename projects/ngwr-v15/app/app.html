How strong should a control's border be?

Everything else on this page is a report — the ramp and the intents are already decided and shipped. The one open question is the last two rows of Borders, outlined in red: the scale's own next step reads 2.56:1, and WCAG 1.4.11 asks 3:1 of anything that identifies a control. Reaching it needs a colour darker than the ramp's own rhythm, so control edges would read visibly heavier than every other line on the page. That is the whole of the trade.

Flip the theme with the button above — the numbers are measured against the live canvas, not transcribed.
